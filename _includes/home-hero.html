01 / Physical activity & aging

Exercise.
Growth. Aging.

We explore physical activity, physiological function, and health across the aging process.

Combining movement assessment and population data, we examine how physical activity relates to cardiovascular, metabolic, and bone health.

02 / Exercise & performance

Training.
Performance.

We investigate how training protocols influence muscle function and fatigue resistance in athletes.

Our work examines exercise intensity, work-to-rest ratios, and recovery to better understand physiological responses to training.

Inside our labSprint timing · Heart rate sensing↗

03 / Cardiovascular responses

Exercise.
Recovery.

We examine heart rate variability and vascular responses to high-intensity interval exercise.

Controlled cycling provides a setting to study exercise intensity, cardiac autonomic regulation, and recovery.

04 / Muscle & joint function

Strength.
Muscle function.

We assess knee flexor and extensor strength and differences between the dominant and non-dominant legs.

Isokinetic assessment helps us examine strength ratios, muscle function, and asymmetry in athletes.

Inside our labCybex Humac Norm · Model 770↗

05 / Body composition & bone

Composition.
Bone health.

We use DXA to assess body composition alongside measurements of muscle function.

Fat mass, lean mass, and bone measurements complement our study of growth, physical performance, and health.
